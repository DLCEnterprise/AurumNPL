What Is Aurum?

A professional marketplace for buying & selling debt portfolios.

Aurum is a secure, invite-only online platform where banks and financial institutions can list loan portfolios they want to sell, and where qualified investors can browse listings, review sensitive documents, and place bids — all within a structured, legally protected process.

Think of it like a private real estate listing site, but for financial assets — with strict access controls, signed agreements before anyone sees confidential data, and a full audit trail of every action. The platform is fully built, deployed, and live as of May 2026 — and has been further elevated with a professional animation layer, personalization system, and polished interaction design.

Phase 5 — Interaction Design

The Feel of a Native iOS Application

The platform previously looked institutional but felt static — every modal snapped open and closed abruptly, buttons had no press feedback, and page navigation felt like reloading a webpage.

That's now been replaced with a full spring animation layer — the same physics-based motion system used by Apple's iOS and premium SaaS products like Linear and Vercel. Every interaction now has a beginning, middle, and end.

Submit Bid / LOI
Spring modal entrance
The bid form scales up from 94% and rises 20px into position. The backdrop blurs and fades in behind it. Closing reverses both in 140ms — no hard cuts.
Empty bid amount
Shake on error
Submitting without an amount shakes the input field left-right four times — the same feedback used by iOS for a wrong passcode. Clear, immediate, hard to miss.
Successful bid
Staggered reveal
The green confirmation card springs into view, then the checkmark icon pops in a fraction of a second later, and the amount text slides in behind it. Three beats, one satisfying moment.
Save a listing
Bookmark bounce
The bookmark icon bounces to 145% of its size and springs back on save — a tactile confirmation that the action registered. Fills with gold on save, empties on remove.
Notification appears
Slide-in, slide-out
Toasts spring in from the bottom-right with a subtle scale effect. When dismissed — manually or automatically — they slide out to the right and the stack closes the gap smoothly.
Navigate to a new page
Spring page transition
Page content fades and rises 8px into position on every route change. The exit is a quick 100ms fade. The result is a sense of forward motion through the application.

How It Works
✓
Instant preview — every change applies to the live interface the moment you tap it, with no save button and no page reload
✓
Persistent across sessions — preferences are saved to the browser and restored automatically on the next visit
✓
Spring-animated drawer — the preferences panel slides in from the right using the same physics-based spring system as the rest of the interface
✓
Zero performance cost — color and density changes are applied via a single CSS attribute on the page root; no components re-render
✓
One-tap reset — a "Reset to defaults" button at the bottom of the preferences panel returns everything to the original state

Phase 5 — Listing Detail

Every Listing Page Now Tells the Full Story Immediately

Before
Key financial figures (UPB, loan count, asking price) buried below the fold in a card section
Bid history badges were plain translucent grey regardless of bid status
NDA sign flow: a checkbox and a button — no indication of what came next
Saving a listing gave no feedback — no confirmation it actually worked
Modals opened and closed instantly — no transition, no sense of depth
After
A metrics strip directly below the title shows UPB, asking price, loan count, and lien position at a glance
Bid status badges are color-coded — gold for pending, green for accepted, red for declined, orange for countered
NDA flow now has a 3-step indicator (Review → Acknowledge → Access) so users always know where they stand
Saving a listing triggers an animated bookmark bounce and a confirmation toast that slides in and out
Every modal springs in with physics and exits with a soft pull-back — the same on desktop and mobile
